DATA NOTE The statewide layer is the USGS 3D Elevation Program (3DEP) bare-earth elevation service. It combines source datasets at different resolutions; a location’s data is not necessarily LiDAR. Terrain DNA samples the 3DEP surface on a small neighborhood grid; its relief and roughness summaries are exploratory, not survey-grade. View collection date, resolution, and confirmed LiDAR coverage in the USGS LiDAR Explorer ↗. HOW TO READ THE LAND

First the points.
Then the place.

Airborne LiDAR sends pulses of light toward the ground and measures their return. Some pulses pass through gaps in vegetation; processing can separate ground returns from the canopy above.

The USGS 3DEP map turns elevation measurements into hillshade, revealing subtle slopes, creek valleys, ridgelines, and the low relief of the coast. Zoom in to explore; check the source explorer to see exactly what data was collected in a location.
