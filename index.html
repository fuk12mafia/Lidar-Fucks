A DIGITAL TERRAIN MODEL
THE SURFACE, WITH THE COVER LIFTED

A DIFFERENT KIND OF MAP

First, the points.
Then, the place.

Airborne LiDAR sends pulses of light toward the ground and measures their return. Some pulses pass through gaps in vegetation; processing can separate ground returns from the canopy above.

The result is a digital elevation model. A hillshade simulates light across that surface, making subtle slopes, channels, and ridgelines legible.
